Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what Verticlip ("Verticlip," "we," "us") collects, how it is used, and your choices. Questions: jthomaselder6@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Your account. Email address, optional name, and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself).

Billing. If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. We receive your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and plan, not your card details.

Content you provide. Video links and files, logo images, the transcripts and clips generated from them, and your brand-kit settings.

Connected accounts. For YouTube we receive your channel ID and title and request the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly permissions. For TikTok we receive your open ID and display name and request user.info.basic and video.upload. We use these only to show which accounts are connected and to upload clips you choose to publish.

Technical data. Server logs such as request times, IP addresses and error messages, used to secure and run the service.

2. How we use information

  • To transcribe your videos, find and score clip moments, and render clips.
  • To upload the clips you select to the accounts you connected.
  • To run your plan, track minutes used, and handle billing.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use YouTube or TikTok user data for advertising or to train AI models.

3. Service providers

To process a video we send its audio to OpenAI for transcription and its transcript to Anthropic for clip scoring. Both process the data to return results to us under their API terms. Payments go through Stripe. Uploads you request go to Google (YouTube) and TikTok.

4. Storage and security

Clips, transcripts and source files are stored on our servers until you delete the project or your account. OAuth tokens for connected accounts are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private.

5. Your choices

  • Delete any project, with its clips and transcript, from the project page.
  • Disconnect a YouTube or TikTok account in Social accounts to delete its stored tokens, or revoke access from your Google or TikTok settings.
  • Email jthomaselder6@gmail.com to delete your account and all its data. We complete deletions within 30 days.

6. Children

Verticlip is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be announced in the app.
