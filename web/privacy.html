1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, optional name, a salted hash of your password (never the password), whether you confirmed your email, and when you accepted our Terms and confirmed your age.
  • Content you provide: video links and uploaded files, logo images, and the transcripts, scores, titles and clips we generate from them, plus your brand-kit settings.
  • Connected accounts: for YouTube, your channel ID and title (permissions youtube.upload and youtube.readonly); for TikTok, your open ID and display name (user.info.basic and video.upload). Access tokens are encrypted at rest. We use this data only to show which accounts are connected and to upload clips you choose to publish.
  • Billing data: Stripe processes payments. We receive your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and your plan, not your card number.
  • Technical data: server logs with request times, IP addresses and error messages, used for security and troubleshooting.
