Questions

What videos work best?

Anything with people talking: podcasts, interviews, streams, talks, tutorials and vlogs. Verticlip reads what's said, so music-only or silent videos won't produce clips.

How long can a video be?

Up to three hours per video. Longer videos simply use more of your monthly minutes.

Can I clip someone else's video?

Only if you own it or have permission. You're responsible for the rights to everything you clip and post.

Does it post without asking me?

Never. Clips only go to YouTube or TikTok when you press Publish. YouTube uploads start private by default, and TikTok clips land in your inbox for a final check.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the billing page. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
