Common problems

"This link can't be used" or the download failed

Verticlip can only fetch public videos. Private, unlisted-with-restrictions, age-restricted, members-only and live streams can't be downloaded. Try another link, or download the file yourself and use Upload file.

"Not enough speech was found"

The video needs clear spoken words. Music-only, silent or very short videos can't be clipped.

"No clip-worthy moments were found"

Try a longer video, or a different clip length. The AI looks for self-contained moments with a strong opening and a payoff.

The video is too long or the file is too large

There is a limit on the length of a source video and on upload size. Trim it and try again.

I didn't get the confirmation or reset email

Check spam. You can ask for a new confirmation link from your dashboard, or request a new reset link on the forgot password page. Links expire, so use the newest one.

My clips are gone

Projects are deleted {{PROJECT_DAYS}} days after you create them, and original videos after {{SOURCE_DAYS}} days. Download the clips you want to keep.

The upload to YouTube or TikTok failed

Reconnect the account in Social accounts and try again. TikTok uploads go to your TikTok inbox for you to finish posting.
