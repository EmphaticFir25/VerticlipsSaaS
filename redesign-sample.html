Which sites can I paste a link from?

YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, Kick and most public video pages. You can also upload a file directly.

What counts as a minute?

Minutes of source video, rounded up. Failed or cancelled projects are refunded automatically.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Manage or cancel from the billing page. You keep your plan until the end of the paid period.
