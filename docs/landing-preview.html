What customers say

From link to posted in three steps

No timeline scrubbing. You review a ranked list of finished clips and publish the ones you like.

  1. Add a video

    Paste a YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo or Kick link, or upload a file up to three hours long.

  2. Verticlip finds the moments

    It transcribes every word, then picks self-contained moments that open with a strong line and end on the payoff.

  3. Review and publish

    Clips come back ranked by score, cropped to 9:16 and captioned. Download them or send them to your channels.

Everything a short needs

Virality score

Every clip is scored from 0 to 100, with a breakdown for hook, flow, value and trend and a one-line reason. Post the strongest first.

Animated captions

Words light up as they are spoken. Pick word highlight, bold box or clean subtitles, in your brand color.

Hook titles

A short on-screen title for the first seconds, written from what is said in the clip.

Fill or fit

Crop to full-screen 9:16, or keep the whole frame on a blurred background for wide shots and screen shares.

Brand kit

Set caption style, color and logo once. Every new project uses them.

Publish to Shorts and TikTok

Connect every channel you run. Each clip gets its own title, description and hashtags.

Clean cuts

Clips start and end on whole sentences, never mid-word, and never overlap.

Questions

Which sites can I paste a link from?

YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, Kick and most public video pages. You can also upload a file.

What counts as a minute?

One minute of source video, rounded up. A one-hour video uses 60 minutes, however many clips come out of it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Manage or cancel from the billing page. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
